# Moderation and Safety Notes

- The Forum shipped here is a client-side mock without moderation. When connecting a real backend, implement:
  - User authentication and account verification.
  - Post/report flags, automated abuse detection, and human moderation flows.
  - Rate-limiting, content filtering for PII, self-harm, or harassment.
- Prominently show crisis resources on every page; make them accessible (keyboard focus, aria-labels).
- Keep logs for moderation and be mindful of privacy and data protection laws in your jurisdiction.
